## Office foreground

`assets/room-graphite-alpha.png`, 1591 × 989 px, RGBA. Generated and retouched with the built-in ImageGen tool. The person and office are synthetic. The city photo was not passed to the generator; the window openings retain transparency.

The foreground is a single raster asset. Hair, clothing, office surfaces, lighting and plants are not individually editable objects. The city photo, wall sign and added vector elements are placed separately.

The original visual reference was supplied with the brief. No general redistribution licence is asserted for that reference.
